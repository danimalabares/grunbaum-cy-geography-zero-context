# Abel mathematical facts and objectives

## Verified facts

- The Grünbaum--Sreedharan Stanley--Reisner threefold has degree (20),
  Hilbert polynomial
  (P(m)=\frac{10}{3}m^3+\frac{14}{3}m), and self-dual resolution
  (1,16,30,16,1) in shifts (0,3,4,5,8).
- Its embedded Hilbert tangent space has dimension (109); the infinitesimal
  projective-coordinate orbit has dimension (56), and the selected
  intrinsic complement has dimension (53).
- The zero-context packet provides an explicit (S_3)-invariant first jet
  and compatible equivariant generator/relation jet through order (q^6).
- Exact recomputation over (mathbb Q) confirms that the first-order
  correction is (T_1y) in the packet's actual basis and equals the
  (q)-coefficient of the stored six-jet.
- The packet proves an abstract all-orders equivariant formal extension and
  an existential algebraization, but supplies no finite algebraic equations
  for a smooth fiber.
- For every smooth fiber established by the packet:
  (H^3=20), (c_2\cdot H=56), (h^{2,1}\le31), and (H) is a primitive
  complete very ample polarization.
- Under the packet/package identification of the computed obstruction output
  with the full embedded Kuranishi equations, the completed local Hilbert
  scheme at the Stanley--Reisner point has dimension at most (94). The 27
  components currently displayed are components only of the quadratic
  obstruction locus, not proved Hilbert components.
- The known general (4\times4) determinantal degree-20 family has Hodge
  numbers ((2,34)) and embedded Hilbert dimension (97), so it cannot
  contain this Stanley--Reisner point under the same local-bound hypothesis.

## Open problems

- Determine the exact smoothing-component dimension and the number of
  smoothing components through the Stanley--Reisner point.
- Compute (h^{1,1}), (h^{2,1}), the Euler characteristic, and the full
  Picard lattice, including torsion.
- Decide whether (H) generates the Picard group.
- Determine the fundamental group and simple-connectedness.
- Identify the Artinian reduction/apolar stratum and compare it with known
  codimension-four Gorenstein lifting strata.
- Determine whether the smooth fiber admits a determinantal, unprojection,
  biliaison, or rational-linkage construction not visible in the special
  monomial presentation.

## Proposed research objectives

- Effectivize Hilbert-scheme curve selection or construct a terminating
  polynomial/algebraic-power-series lift from the verified six-jet.
- Certify a concrete fiber by exact flatness, special-fiber, dimension,
  degree, and saturated-Jacobian checks.
- Compute the tangent/obstruction theory at that smooth fiber and thereby
  determine (h^{2,1}) and its Hilbert component.
- Compute Picard and topological invariants using a concrete geometric model,
  monodromy, or a controlled semistable/toroidal replacement of the
  degeneration.
- Search systematically for codimension-four Gorenstein formats and explicit
  liaison chains, with particular attention to a rational residual variety.
- Use the inherited (S_3)-action to study fixed loci, equivariant Hodge
  data, and constraints on the Picard representation.
