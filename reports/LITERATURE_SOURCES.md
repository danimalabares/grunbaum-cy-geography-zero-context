## CGKK 2016

- Citation: S. Coughlan, Ł. Gołębiowski, G. Kapustka, M. Kapustka,
  *Arithmetically Gorenstein Calabi--Yau threefolds in P7*.
- Stable source: [arXiv:1609.01195](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.01195), v1.
- Inspection state: `inspected-primary`.
- Locations: Table 1; Problem 3.1; Section 4.9.
- Used statement: the listed degree-20 family is defined by (3\times3)
  minors of a (4\times4) linear matrix and has Hodge numbers ((2,34));
  completeness for degrees 18--20 was presented as evidence/open problem.
- Applicability: comparison family only; not an identification of the
  zero-context smoothing.

## Bertin 2007/2009

- Citation: M.-A. Bertin, *Examples of Calabi--Yau 3-folds of P7 with
  rho=1*, Canadian J. Math. 61 (2009), 1050--1072.
- Stable source: [arXiv:math/0701511](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701511), v1;
  DOI `10.4153/CJM-2009-050-2`.
- Inspection state: `inspected-primary` for the arXiv record and construction
  scope; the detailed degree-20 invariant is cross-referenced through CGKK.
- Used statement: the codimension-four examples use Gulliksen--Negård and
  Kustin--Miller complexes and include a degree-20 construction.
- Applicability: historical source for the known family only.

## Kapustka--Kapustka--Ranestad--Schenck--Stillman--Yuan

- Citation: *Quaternary quartic forms and Gorenstein rings*.
- Stable source: [arXiv:2111.05817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05817), v1;
  Memoirs AMS 317 (2026).
- Inspection state: `inspected-primary`.
- Locations: Introduction, especially the discussion of type `[000]`, and
  Section 8 references.
- Used statement: smooth degree-20 examples of this Betti type arise as
  (4\times4) submaximal-minor liftings of special `[000]` Artinian forms;
  general `[000]` data have more restrictive lifting behavior.
- Applicability: motivates an apolar-stratum comparison; it does not prove
  that the zero-context fiber is determinantal or classify its component.
