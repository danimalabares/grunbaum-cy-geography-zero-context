## 1. Normalized invariant generators

Let S=Q[a,...,h]. Let f_i, i=1,...,16, have the immutable order in the
question. There are120 cubic monomials and104 cubic monomials outside
I_M. Use all of these104 tails, not only tails smaller in a term order:

    F_i=f_i+sum_m c_(i,m)m.

This is the ordinary affine chart of the Grassmannian of sixteen-dimensional
cubic subspaces with the original sixteen monomials as pivots. It imposes
no Gröbner degeneration or coherence assumption. In particular no claim
that the nonpolytopal sphere is a coherent monomial degeneration is hidden.

The group permutes generator-tail pairs. Invariance is exactly equality
of the coefficients on its orbits. There are291 orbits, enumerated
deterministically in `data/fixed_chart.json`; call their coefficients z_j.
This removes generator-basis freedom while preserving every invariant
embedded deformation in this local chart.

## 2. Multiplication and all the first syzygies

**PROVED:** the degree-four multiplication matrix

    mu_F : S_1 tensor Q^16 -> S_4

has size330×128. At F0, its128 columns are monomials, with98 distinct
images. Choose the first column producing each distinct image; each
remaining column gives a difference with its representative. Thus the
kernel has30 explicit independent linear syzygies. Since the special
resolution has all first syzygies in degree4, these30 generate the entire
graded special first-syzygy module, not merely one graded slice.

Order the98 special ideal monomials before the232 standard quartics, and
the98 representative columns before the30 extra columns. Then

    mu_F = [ A  B ]       A is98×98, B is98×30,
           [ C  D ]       C is232×98, D is232×30.

At the origin A=identity, C=D=0, and each column of B0 has one1.
On det(A)≠0, define U=A^(-1)B. The matrix R with representative rows
−U and extra rows the identity specializes to the full syzygy basis.
The equation FR=0 is precisely

    E=D-C A^(-1)B=0,                       232×30=6960 equations.

Avoid computing A^(-1) symbolically. Put U=U0+W and retain the equations
A(U0+W)=B. These are2940 equations linear in W over the generator
coefficient ring. Their differential in W at the origin is the identity.

## 3. Why this is a Hilbert chart, not a rank heuristic

**PROVED:** work in the local coefficient ring at the special point.
The equations give exact lifted syzygies whose special reductions generate
all the special syzygies. If M=S_B/(F), then reduction of
S_B^16→(F)→0 modulo the maximal ideal identifies (F) tensor k with I_M:
every relation in the special presentation has already lifted. The
sequence0→(F)→S_B→M→0 therefore gives Tor_1^B(M,k)=0.
In each homogeneous degree M_d is finite over B, hence flat and free.
This is the finite-module local flatness criterion; see
[Stacks, Lemma10.99.7, tag00MK](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00MK).
Consequently the exact incidence equations define a flat graded family
locally, with the required Hilbert function in every degree.

Conversely a flat embedded infinitesimal deformation in this cubic chart
has degree-three ideal rank16, degree-four image rank98 and kernel rank30.
The same invertible pivot determines its syzygy matrix uniquely. Nakayama,
or the source's postulation comparison, ensures its ideal is generated by
the sixteen lifted cubics. Thus the completed incidence chart is the
completed fixed Hilbert germ. No higher-degree Gotzmann chart is needed.

## 5. Precisely which six-jet is preserved

Let H(q) be the certified generic equivariant generator row through q6.
Let P(q) be its16×16 coefficient matrix on the special pivot monomials;
P(0)=identity. Replace

    H -> H P^(-1),            R -> P R.

This is an invertible generator-basis change, with no coordinate change
or reparametrization. The script computes P^(-1) exactly modulo q7 over
Q, verifies the pivot normalization and all coefficient-orbit equalities,
and records the inverse. It then assigns to the21 free z_j exactly their
normalized degree-six polynomials w_j(q). All subsequent free coefficients
are set to zero: this chooses a new, unambiguous algebraic curve.

Set these21 coordinates equal to w_j(q) in the270 selected equations,
and retain the2940 equations solving U. The result is3210 finite
polynomial equations in q,270 dependent z variables, and2940 W variables.
The point is q=z=W=0. Its derivative in the3210 dependent variables is
invertible. The unique local branch is an algebraic curve, étale over
the q-line at0. Its function field is finite over Q(q).

**PROVED:** formal uniqueness makes its normalized generator ideal agree
with the certified ideal modulo q7. Syzygy frames need not agree: after
normalizing the known source R, its30 columns and our30 columns span
the same kernel modulo q7, so solve the degree-one128×30 coefficient
matrices for a30×30 basis matrix and check its constant determinant is
nonzero. Alternatively equality of normalized generator rows already
proves equality of the embedded six-jet ideals. Exact matrix equality
of R is not required for lineage.

This is category2 (same embedded six-jet), not category1 (same all-orders
arc). The source never singled out a unique all-orders continuation.

## 6. Integral continuation and generic smoothness

**PROVED, conditional on the existing smoothness certificates:** all
normalizing denominators are units in Z_(101), and the new dependent
Jacobian determinant is−1. The implicit formal solution exists over
Z_(101)[[free coordinates]]. All the omitted equations vanish over Q;
torsion-freeness embeds this series ring into Q[[free coordinates]], so
they also vanish integrally. Substitute the integral w_j(q).

An invertible generator-basis change preserves the ideal. Modulo that
ideal it also preserves the Jacobian rank/Fitting ideal, because the
basis-change matrix is independent of the projective coordinates.
Consequently this continuation has the same finite smoothness witnesses
modulo101. The accepted properness argument transfers generic smoothness
to characteristic zero. The algebraic component through the marked point
therefore has smooth geometric generic fibre.

Other components of the displayed polynomial incidence scheme away from
the marked germ are not certified. Isolate the origin's component, or
retain the local/étale branch selector, in every elimination. At a chosen
finite q, avoid denominator/discriminant loci and separately certify the
actual fibre; do not specialize a truncated series.

## 7. Computational advantage and next reduction

The recurrence factors the same270×270 scalar matrix once. At order n,
the only new unknowns are270 generator coefficients; U_n then follows
from A(0)=identity. Check all6960 equations, not just270. Each completed
order is saved. Mod101 coefficients are bounded; no growing multivariate
Gröbner basis is constructed. The daytime order6 regression took7.4s
including normalization, with sampled peak RSS about28MB.

30-minute/2.8GB guard is a ceiling, not a prediction. Try small common
rational denominators for all generator and syzygy coefficients, then
verify the full polynomial FR identity. Failure of that ansatz leaves
the finite algebraic curve intact.
