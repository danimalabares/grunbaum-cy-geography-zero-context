# Corrections and clarifications to `runs/kapustka-degree20-comparison-2026-09-11/`

The historical run is left byte-for-byte unchanged (its `HASH_MANIFEST.json` still verifies).
None of the items below reverses a conclusion of that run; they sharpen statements, fill gaps in
proofs, or add the missing published theorem. Items are ordered by importance.

1. **Which morphism is "the contraction" (`REPORT.md` §1.2, `DANI_EXPLANATION.md` §1).** Both
   write `φ = φ_{|G|} : X → Ybar` for the primitive type II contraction. Kapustka himself
   distinguishes the two: `φ_{|G|}(X)` is the non-normal threefold `Y` (S1, Prop. 3.2 and
   Rem. 3.3: "The threefold Y is not normal at P. We need to take a multiple of G to obtain a
   primitive contraction"), and the primitive contraction is `φ_{|2G|}` (S1, Thm. 3.4; the proof
   of Thm. 5.1 "conclude[s] as in the proof of Theorem 3.4"). The run's `Ybar` is the normal image
   of `φ_{|2G|}` (equivalently the Stein factorisation of `φ_{|G|}`), and `T` is the ample Cartier
   divisor with `φ*T = G`, which Kapustka calls "the image T of G", noting "2T is very ample".
   The run's mathematics is about the right objects; the notation `φ_{|G|}` for the normal
   contraction is the imprecision. Kapustka's Remark 3.3 is in fact a published statement of the
   run's claim E (non-normality of the `P⁷`-image) for the analogous degree-15 row.

2. **Missing theorem behind the analytic model (`REPORT.md` Prop. 4.1, K05 in `PROOFS.md`).** The
   run asserts `gr_{m_P} Ô_{Ybar,P} = ⊕ H⁰(D', −nK_{D'})` and "analytically
   `(Ybar,P) ≅ ({xy − zw = 0} ⊂ A⁴)/±1`" from the formal-functions computation alone. Two points were
   skipped: (a) the identification of the `m_P`-adic filtration with the order-of-vanishing
   filtration along `D'` needs the anticanonical ring of `P¹×P¹` to be generated in degree one
   together with Artin–Rees cofinality (otherwise one only gets `dim T_P ≥ 9`, which is all the
   `P⁷` obstruction needs); (b) the *analytic* isomorphism with the cone is a theorem: Gross,
   *Deforming Calabi–Yau threefolds*, Prop. 5.4 (via Grauert's linearisation theorem), whose
   hypothesis "the blow-up of `P` is the contraction" is Reid, *Canonical 3-folds*, Thm. 2.11, as
   quoted in the proof of Gross's Thm. 5.2. `SOURCES.md` of the run cites Gross only for Thm. 5.8
   (smoothability). `DANI_NOTE.tex` §2 supplies both.

3. **The local ring of `Y` at `P` (`REPORT.md` §4.2, proof of 3 and 4).** "The local ring of Y at
   P is the subring of `Ô_{Ybar,P}` generated by the images of the seven sections […], i.e. the
   coordinate ring of the cone over the projected surface `S`" is not literally correct: the seven
   functions have higher-order terms, so the subring is not a graded cone ring. What is correct,
   and gives the same `δ = 2`, is that the subring's associated graded ring for the induced
   filtration is `k ⊕ V ⊕ A₂ ⊕ A₃ ⊕ …` (`V` the 7-dimensional span of the linear forms, `A` the
   anticanonical ring), using `h¹(I_S(k)) = 0` for `k ≥ 2`. `DANI_NOTE.tex` Prop. 3.1.

4. **Characteristic.** The run's `h¹(I_S(k))` values, Betti table and Hilbert polynomial of `Y`
   were computed over `F₃₂₀₀₃` only (its `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` (vi) says so). The Betti table and
   `h¹(I_S(k))` are now verified over `QQ` for a random integer centre (`logs/c03`), which by
   semicontinuity settles them for the general centre in characteristic zero. `dim (I_Y)₂ = 2`
   remains finite-field only; the note uses only `≥ 2`.

5. **Reduction theorem (`REPORT.md` Thm. 5.1).** Part (3) (fibres of an *embedded* smoothing are
   arithmetically Gorenstein with h-vector `(1,4,10,4,1)`) is re-proved in `DANI_NOTE.tex`
   Prop. 4.1 with the constant-Hilbert-function step made explicit (semicontinuity of
   `h⁰(I(k))` and of `h⁰(O(k))` together with `H¹(I_{SR(M)}(k)) = 0`). Parts (1)–(2), the passage
   from an *abstract* smoothing to an embedded one, were **not re-verified**; the note says so and
   notes that the smoothing under review is embedded by construction, so the abstract statement is
   not needed for it. Status labels in `PROOFS.md` §8 are not changed.

6. **Terminology.** The run says `T` "is not very ample on Ybar" and that `Ybar` "needs
   `P(1⁸,2²)`". The note phrases the second point through the section ring
   `R = ⊕ H⁰(Ybar, nT)` (two generators in degree 2) and adds the distinction the task asked for:
   `R` is a Gorenstein ring, so `Ybar` is "arithmetically Gorenstein" in `Proj R`, but the notion
   relevant to `SR(M)` is arithmetically Gorenstein *in `P⁷`*, which fails for `Ybar` (not even a
   subscheme) and for `Y` (not normal, Hilbert polynomial `P(n) − 2`).

7. **What is not touched.** The first-order obstruction (`REPORT.md` §7, Prop. 7.1) and the local
   all-orders statement were neither re-derived nor contradicted here; the note refers to them as
   claims of the earlier run. The decision of `(★)` remains open exactly as in the run's §8.3.
